Privacy Policy

GIF Background Remover — a Windows app by Rhizkit. Effective date: 23 September 2026.

Short version: GIF Background Remover runs entirely on your PC. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Besides the transparent GIFs you ask it to create, the only file it writes is a small local preference file with your last-used settings.

What the app accesses

GIF Background Remover only touches the files you explicitly point it at:

  • It reads the GIF files you add to the queue, to detect their background color, show a preview, and remove the background.
  • It writes new files named name_transparent.gif — next to the original, or in a folder you choose. Your original GIFs are never modified, moved, or deleted.

That is the full extent of its file access. It does not scan your drives, read other documents, or touch anything you did not select.

What is stored on your PC

The app keeps two small things locally, both under %LOCALAPPDATA%\GifBgRemover\ (on a Microsoft Store install, this is redirected inside the app's own package data folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\):

  • A tiny config.json file remembering your settings (background mode, custom color, tolerance, clean-edges value) and the output folder you chose, if any. It contains no image content and no personal information beyond a folder path you selected yourself.
  • A local cache used only to render the app's own interface (the interface is a local web view).

Both can be cleared at any time by resetting the app from Windows Settings, or by deleting that folder.

What the app does NOT do

  • It makes no internet connections — there is no server for it to talk to. The optional "View in Store" button only asks Windows to open the Microsoft Store app on another Rhizkit product page; the app itself sends nothing.
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or third-party SDK.
  • It does not capture your screen, read your clipboard, or log keystrokes.
  • It does not upload your GIFs anywhere. All processing happens on your machine.

Third parties

None. GIF Background Remover shares data with no one, because it collects no data and has no connection over which to share it. The app relies on the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (a standard Windows component from Microsoft) to draw its interface; it sends nothing over the network on this app's behalf.

Children

GIF Background Remover collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
