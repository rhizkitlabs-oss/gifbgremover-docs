GIF Background Remover

A free Windows app by Rhizkit. Make the solid background of your GIF animations transparent — a whole folder at a time, entirely on your own PC.

Get it free on the Microsoft Store

What it does

  • Auto-detects the background — reads the color around the edges of each GIF, so a batch of black, white, and colored backgrounds just works.
  • Keeps your artwork intact — only background connected to the edge of the frame is removed. Dark shadows and details inside your subject stay, even when they match the background color.
  • Clean edges — trim the thin fringe that GIF transparency can leave around a subject, with a live animated before/after preview.
  • Batch everything — add files or whole folders. Results are saved as name_transparent.gif; your originals are never modified.
100% offline: no account, no upload, no subscription. Your GIFs never leave your PC.
